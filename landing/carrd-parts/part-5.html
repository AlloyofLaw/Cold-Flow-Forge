Straight answers

Questions you're right to ask.

Will this burn my domain?

No. We send from dedicated domains built just for this — set up right with SPF, DKIM and DMARC. We keep spam complaints under the 0.10% line Google, Yahoo and Microsoft now enforce. Your main domain — the one on your website and invoices — never sends cold email. Ever.

What counts as a "qualified" call?

A real conversation with a company that fits your ICP, has a problem you can actually help with, and is worth your time — and actually shows up. Booked-but-no-show doesn't count. That's the number nobody else reports.

Won't your dashboard just look healthy while nothing lands?

That's the oldest trick in the category. A green dashboard hiding a 0% real reply rate. So we report held rates — calls that actually happened. The one number that's easy to fake is the one we lead with.

Why not just hire a salesperson?

Do the math. One SDR runs about $60,000 a year, takes three months to ramp, and often quits inside a year — before booking a single call. With us there's no salary and no ramp. After your first 5 calls, you only pay when a qualified call shows up.

What does it cost?

Fair question — and the honest answer is it varies. The right setup depends on your market, how big your list needs to be, and the volume it takes to hit your numbers — so there's no one-size price to post here. What doesn't change: no retainer, no maintenance fee, your first 5 qualified calls are baked into the setup, and after that you only pay when a real qualified call actually shows up. We walk through the exact numbers together on the call, once we both know it's a fit. The audit itself is free.

Is cold email even legal?

Done right, yes. We follow CAN-SPAM and GDPR: a real sender name, proper setup (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), one-click unsubscribe on every email, opt-outs honored right away. We respect the rules. This isn't a scraped-list blast.
