Privacy Policy

EFFECTIVE: JULY 6, 2026

Cold Flow Forge (“we”, “us”) runs controlled outbound programs for B2B agencies. This page explains what information we collect through this website and our outreach, and how we handle it.

Information we collect on this site

  • Booking details. When you book a call through the Calendly widget on this site, Calendly collects the details you enter (name, email, and anything you add to the booking form) and shares them with us so we can hold the meeting.
  • Email. If you email us at andrew@coldflowforge.com, we keep the correspondence so we can reply and follow up.

We don't run our own analytics, ad pixels, or tracking cookies on this site.

Third-party services

This site is built on Carrd and embeds a small number of third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Calendly — scheduling widget and booking data.
  • YouTube — the video player loads only if you press play, and uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode.
  • Google Fonts — typefaces served by Google.

Outreach we send on behalf of clients

Our business is business-to-business email outreach. Contact details used in campaigns come from publicly available business information and licensed B2B data providers, and messages are sent to work addresses in a professional context. Every message identifies the sender, and if you ask to stop hearing from us — by replying or using the opt-out in the email — we suppress your address immediately and permanently.

How we use information

  • To hold the calls you book and respond to your messages.
  • To run and improve outreach campaigns for our clients.
  • We do not sell personal information.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Email andrew@coldflowforge.com and we'll handle it promptly.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with an updated effective date.
