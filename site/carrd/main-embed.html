Feast or famine

One month you're slammed and the bank balance looks great. The next month? Crickets.

The work still gets delivered. Clients are happy. The team is busy. But new business has gone quiet:

  • A referral slows down.
  • A warm lead goes cold.
  • An old prospect says “maybe next quarter.”
  • One big client pauses the budget — or takes it in-house.

It doesn't feel like a crisis yet. That's the trap. Then the silence starts to cost you, and you're staring at the inbox doing the math you hate: where is the next client actually coming from?

“Referrals are like rain. Great when it comes — but you don't control the weather.”

That's not a pipeline. It's a waiting game with a nicer dashboard. Hope can carry a slow week. It won't cover payroll — and it won't build an agency you can count on.

Before you book

Fair questions, straight answers.

If there's no big retainer, how do you actually get paid?

Our compensation is tied to qualified calls that book and hold — not a flat monthly fee you pay regardless of results. The exact structure depends on your offer and deal size, and we walk through it openly on the audit call. The short version: we don't get paid properly unless your calendar fills with the right people.

Will this put my domain or sender reputation at risk?

No — because your main domain never sends a single cold email. All outreach runs on separate, dedicated sending domains with SPF, DKIM and DMARC configured, warmed properly, and kept under the <0.10% spam-complaint threshold that Google, Yahoo and Microsoft enforce. If a sending domain ever degrades, it gets retired — your brand's domain is never the crash-test dummy.

What exactly counts as a “qualified” call?

Before launch, we agree on it in writing: the company profile, the role of the person (a real decision-maker, not an intern), and the fit criteria that make a call worth your time. A call only counts when it matches that definition and the person actually shows up. No-shows and tire-kickers don't count toward anything.

How fast will I see calls on my calendar?

The first stretch is deliberately unglamorous: sharpening your ICP, building the list, and setting up and warming the sending infrastructure so emails actually land in inboxes. Rushing that step is how domains get burned. From there, the target is the one on this page — 15–20 qualified calls within 90 days. Anyone promising a full calendar in week one is either guessing or spamming.

How much of my time does this take?

It's front-loaded: onboarding, a deep-dive on your offer and best clients, and your sign-off on the messaging that will carry your name. After launch, your main job is showing up to the calls that get booked. We handle the sending, the inbox, and the weekly optimization.

What if outbound just isn't right for my agency?

Then we'll tell you that on the audit call — for free, before anything gets built. Sometimes the offer needs sharpening first, or the leak is after calls book, not before. If outbound isn't the right move yet, you'll leave knowing what to fix first instead of paying to find out the hard way.
